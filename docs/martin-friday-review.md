## The five steps

1. Cross-check. One of us produces the work. The other checks the actual output against Daniel's request, with the file or message opened, before seeing the author's self-score. Skip the blind order when it would hold up urgent work, and say that the check was not blind.
2. Record a failure or redo once. Include the source, a confirmed or unknown cause, the owner, and a test that would catch it next time. Keep a changed requirement separate from a defect.
3. Trial at most one new process change each week. Compare it with the old approach on the same suitable historical or synthetic examples, and include one fresh example. Report accepted handovers over eligible completed handovers, and actual rework where it was recorded. Do not assume time saved, and do not claim the change caused the result from a small sample.
4. Keep, refine, or retire the rule from those results. "Written into this routine" and "shown to prevent a repeat" stay separate states. If the sample is too small, mark the evidence insufficient and carry the trial.
5. Friday recap to Daniel. Count new issues, repeats, older lessons, and rules, and label unknown causes as unknown. Include rules tried and evidence still missing. A quiet week is limited activity, not a perfect week. Do not invent scores.

Steps 2 and 5 were already how the Friday review ran. Steps 1, 3, and 4 were added on 10 November 2026 and are saved in this file.
